Response to the Second Referee Report -- DT18474/Kim

We thank the Referee for the careful second reading and for the endorsement of publication.  We are glad the treatment of telescope optics now meets the concern raised in the first report.

The Referee identifies four places where "reads" is used actively in a way that could confuse.  All four have been replaced with more descriptive wording/phrasing, though not exactly as proposed by the Referee.  They are marked in the accompanying changes-marked copy of the manuscript; no other text has been altered.

We are grateful to the Referee for both reports, which have improved the
paper considerably.
